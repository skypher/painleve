# Positivity Roadmap

## Current scope

For now, the sole target is the positivity theorem:

> **Theorem P.** Let \(a>0\), set \(\epsilon=a^{-2}\), and let
> \(v_n=0\) for \(n<0\).  The solution determined by
> \[
> v_0=-\frac1a\frac{h'(a)}{h(a)},\qquad
> v_1=-\frac1a\frac{h''(a)}{h'(a)}
> \]
> and
> \[
> v_n\!\left(
> 1+v_{n-1}v_{n-2}+v_{n+1}v_{n-1}+v_{n+2}v_{n+1}
> \right)=(n+1)\epsilon
> \]
> satisfies \(v_n(a)>0\) for every \(n\geq0\).

Uniqueness, closed determinant formulas, and asymptotic expansions are not
current deliverables.  Determinants, differential identities, or endpoint
bounds may be used only when they directly prove positivity.

## 1. Normalize the problem

1. Verify the scaling \(V_n=a v_n\) and \(\epsilon=a^{-2}\) from the
   quartic orthogonal-polynomial recurrence.
2. Verify the Bessel formula for \(h\), the differential equation
   \[
   h'''(a)+a h(a)+a^2h'(a)=0,
   \]
   and the formulas for \(v_0,v_1\).
3. Derive the forward form
   \[
   v_{n+2}
   =
   \frac{(n+1)\epsilon/v_n-1-v_{n-1}v_{n-2}
   -v_{n+1}v_{n-1}}{v_{n+1}}.
   \]
   This identifies the exact inequality that must remain positive at every
   step.

**Output:** a self-contained normalization lemma with every sign and index
checked.

## 2. Prove the initial sign package

1. Prove \(h(a)>0\) from the positive-definite quadratic form in
   \(J_{-1/4}(a^2/4)\) and \(J_{1/4}(a^2/4)\).
2. Prove \(h'(a)<0\) and \(h''(a)>0\), hence \(v_0,v_1>0\).
3. Prove the sharper inequalities required by the first recurrence steps,
   beginning with
   \[
   0<v_0<\epsilon,
   \]
   which is equivalent to \(v_2>0\).
4. Continue only as far as needed to reveal a stable inductive inequality;
   isolated verification of many small indices is not a substitute for the
   all-\(n\) argument.

**Output:** positivity of the initial coefficients and a candidate invariant
inequality.

## 3. Find the all-index positivity mechanism

### Route A: recurrence and differential inequalities

1. Differentiate the orthogonal-polynomial norm ratios with respect to \(a\)
   and derive the exact coupled differential system for \(V_n(a)\).
2. Formulate a forward-invariant region using the numerator in the forward
   recurrence and any adjacent-product inequalities forced by the system.
3. Assume a first loss of positivity and track the coupled behavior of
   \(v_n,v_{n+1},v_{n+2}\).  A scalar zero-crossing argument is insufficient
   because a zero can be accompanied by a later pole.
4. Rule out every possible first zero/pole configuration.

### Route B: minimal Gram-sign argument

1. Express \(v_n\) as the ratio of consecutive signed orthogonal-polynomial
   norms.
2. Use the \(C_4\) symmetry to split the relevant Gram matrix into four
   residue-class blocks.
3. Derive only the adjacent-minor or block identity needed to preserve the
   expected sign.  Do not pursue general closed determinant formulas.
4. Show that a first vanishing norm contradicts that identity or a
   sign-regularity property of the block kernel.

### Decision rule

After the differential system and the first block identities are explicit,
continue with whichever route supplies an all-index sign invariant.  A route
that produces formulas without a sign mechanism does not advance the target.

**Output:** one proved theorem excluding a first nonpositive coefficient for
all \(a>0\) and all indices.

## 4. Assemble and audit Theorem P

1. Combine the initial sign package with the all-index mechanism.
2. Check that the argument covers the full interval \(a\in(0,\infty)\) and
   every \(n\geq0\), without an unstated limiting assumption.
3. Verify every Bessel identity, differentiation under an integral, limit
   passage, and cited theorem used in the proof.
4. State only the positivity conclusion.  Leave uniqueness and supplementary
   determinant or asymptotic results outside the theorem.

## Completed positivity route

The all-index sign mechanism is now proved in `positivity_proof.md`.
The positive Dixon--Ferrar representation of \(h\) extends, after adding two
quartic source variables, to four positive kernels, one for each \(C_4\)
residue class.  A triangular factorization reduces every finite block to
the bi-moment matrix generated by
\[
s,\qquad f(s)=\frac{s^2}{1+s^2}.
\]
Both functions are strictly increasing, so Andréief's identity fixes the
strict sign of every block.  The resulting signed norms satisfy
\[
h_n>0,\qquad v_n=\frac{h_{n+1}}{a h_n}>0
\]
for all \(n\geq0\).

The completion audit checked the source differentiation, all phase factors,
the four radial kernels, the norm-sign quotient, and the identification with
the forward recurrence.  The positivity-only target is complete in
`positivity_proof.md`.
