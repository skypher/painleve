Why this is a good target
It is genuinely open: Felder and Hoppe prove the corresponding result for degrees d=1,2,3d=1,2,3d=1,2,3, but leave d=4d=4d=4 as the first unsolved case of their Gram-matrix nondegeneracy conjecture. Felder–Hoppe
The entire problem is one scalar recurrence plus an explicit Bessel function—no giant framework needs to be developed.
It comes directly from quantizing the minimal surface z2=z13z_2=z_1^3z2=z13. The matrix equations occur as static-membrane equations and large-NNN string matrix models. Clarkson–Dzhamay–Hone–Mitchell
Positivity would prove that all the relevant orthogonal polynomials exist and that every finite-NNN complex normal-matrix partition function is nonzero.
It is the natural next case after the previously solved quantum parabola z2=z12z_2=z_1^2z2=z12, not an artificial variation.
A clean proof should comfortably support a math-ph arXiv paper and likely a journal submission. A plausible title is:

The positive quartic solution of a higher discrete Painlevé equation from quantum minimal surfaces
Likely attack
The strongest route appears to be:
Use the C4C_4C4 symmetry to split the indefinite Gram matrix into four Hankel-type blocks.
Express their principal minors as Bessel/Wronskian determinants built from h,h′,h′′,…h,h',h'',\ldotsh,h′,h′′,….
Prove the required sign pattern using generalized Turán inequalities or a sign-regularity/total-positivity argument.
Translate the determinant signs into vn>0v_n>0vn>0.
A second possible route is to derive differential equations in aaa for the vnv_nvn and construct a forward-invariant positive region, generalizing the elegant d=3d=3d=3 proof. The obstacle is that the quartic recurrence couples five consecutive terms, so the cubic zero-crossing argument does not transfer automatically.

I also ran a preliminary arbitrary-precision check at representative aaa from 0.010.010.01 to 300300300, through n=100n=100n=100; all computed terms remained positive. That is only reconnaissance, but there is no immediate small-nnn counterexample. Raw forward iteration becomes extremely ill-conditioned for large aaa, making the determinant formulation especially attractive.

The minimum publishable result is the full d=4d=4d=4 positivity theorem. Uniqueness, determinant formulas, or asymptotics would strengthen it but are not essential.
